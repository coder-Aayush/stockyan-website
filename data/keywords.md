# StockYan SEO keyword research

Source: Google autocomplete for Nepal, pulled 27 Oct 2026 with
`https://suggestqueries.google.com/complete/search?client=firefox&hl=en&gl=np&q=<seed>`.
Autocomplete shows what people in Nepal actually type. It gives no volumes, so "strength" below
means how many related suggestions a seed returned and how close they are to our product.

## What the data says overall

- `nepse a-z` is dominated by navigation queries we cannot win: `nepse alpha`, `tms nepse 58 login`,
  `meroshare login`, `nepse index today`, `nepse live`. We target the feature and how-to queries next to them.
- The strongest feature clusters: portfolio tracker, floorsheet, IPO result (bulk), paper trading,
  calculator (WACC, tax, sell), technical analysis chart, broker analysis, heatmap, accumulation/distribution.
- Indicator queries (`rsi`, `macd`, `bollinger bands`, `support and resistance`, `candlestick patterns`)
  are global, but many have NEPSE or Nepal variants (`rsi indicator nepse`, `nepse candlestick pattern`,
  `candlestick patterns pdf in nepali`, `nepse support and resistance`). These become learn pages.
- Competitor names appear often (`nepse alpha ...`, `chukul`, `sharesansar`, `merolagani`, `nepse ai apk`).
  We do not target competitor brand names.

### Why each feature page is worth having

- **nepse-portfolio-tracker**: the cleanest commercial query in the set (`nepse portfolio tracker free`,
  `best nepse portfolio tracker`). The app's portfolio does sync, sector split, dividends and tax on exit.
- **meroshare-portfolio**: MeroShare is the biggest navigational brand in Nepal investing. People search how to
  manage and see their MeroShare portfolio, and `mero share portfolio not showing` is a real pain point we can answer.
  TMS file import is folded in here because every `tms ...` query is a broker login, not a feature search.
- **bulk-ipo-result**: very strong cluster (`bulk ipo result` returned 10 suggestions). Families apply on many
  BOIDs and check results the same day. Free in the app.
- **nepse-stock-screener**: `nepse screener` appears in the `nepse s` list. Hub page for the 14 scanners.
- **nepse-rsi-scanner**: RSI is the most searched indicator with a NEPSE variant (`nepse rsi`, `nepse rsi today`,
  `rsi indicator nepse`). Live intraday RSI is a strong differentiator.
- **nepse-technical-analysis-chart**: `nepse chart` and `nepse technical analysis` each return 10 suggestions,
  many for free/live charts and TradingView. Covers the advanced chart, levels and the desktop Workspace.
- **nepse-broker-analysis**: `broker analysis nepse`, `broker holding nepse`, `top broker holding nepse`.
- **nepse-accumulation-distribution**: `accumulation distribution nepse` and `broker accumulation nepse`.
  Free tool, and an existing learn article (`use-of-accumulation-distribution-data`) supports it.
- **nepse-floorsheet**: one of the most typed NEPSE features (`nepse floorsheet` is in the top 10 for `nepse`).
- **nepse-bulk-transactions**: small but exact match; cheap to rank and it is a free tool.
- **nepse-heatmap**: `nepse heat map live` and `stock heatmap nepse`. We have a strong desktop screenshot (mac4).
- **nepse-paper-trading**: large cluster, including `for pc`, `ios`, `app download`. Built into StockYan.
- **nepse-share-calculator**: biggest how-much cluster (`nepse calculator`, `share calculator nepal` each return
  10 suggestions: sell, tax, WACC, bonus, right, SIP). The app has buy, sell, adjustment, SIP, Graham and averaging tabs.
- **nepse-app-for-pc**: `nepse app for pc`, `meroshare app for pc`, `nepse paper trading for pc`. StockYan has
  real desktop apps; most competitors are websites.
- **nepse-ai-analysis**: `nepse ai` returns 10 suggestions (some are a competitor app's name). The AI Watchlist
  is a real, explainable 0 to 100 score. Copy avoids any "signal" or prediction claims.
- **nepse-alerts**: `nepse alert` and `ipo alert nepal`. Smaller, but alerts are a core retention feature.

### Why each learn page is worth having

- Indicator pages (RSI, MACD, support and resistance, moving averages, Bollinger Bands, Fibonacci, candlesticks)
  each returned a full list of 10 suggestions. Beginner questions ("what is", "explained", "for beginners",
  "how to use") are common, so the pages are written as plain explainers with a real NABIL example from
  27 Oct 2026 and a link to the matching scanner.
- `what-is-floorsheet`: `what is floorsheet in nepse` and `floor sheet meaning` are exact beginner questions.
- Commission, capital gains tax and WACC are Nepal-only questions with clear answers. They feed the calculator
  and portfolio pages.

## Considered and left out

- **TMS import as its own page**: every `tms ...` query is a broker login (`tms nepse 58 login`). TMS file
  import is covered on `meroshare-portfolio`.
- **Volume / volume spike**: `nepse volume` exists, but suggestions are for daily turnover numbers, and
  `volume in stock market` is India-heavy. Volume is covered inside the screener and RSI pages.
- **Accumulation/distribution learn page**: the site already has `use-of-accumulation-distribution-data`.
- **Dividend**: `nepse dividend history`, `upcoming dividend nepse` are data lookups (dividend calendars).
  The app has dividend leaderboards, but a feature page would need live data to rank. Revisit later.
- **Market depth, nepse live, nepse today, nepse index**: navigational/live-data queries owned by NEPSE and news
  sites. A static page cannot answer "today".
- **Best NEPSE app / share market app nepal**: belongs to the homepage, not a feature page.
- **Smart money (SMC), Dow theory, pivot points, nepse fibonacci, nepse bollinger, nepse moving average**:
  no or almost no NEPSE autocomplete. Covered inside the generic learn pages.
- **Circuit breaker, margin trading**: already covered by existing learn articles.
